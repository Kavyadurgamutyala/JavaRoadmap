INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

1. What is Collection?

A collection is a framework used to store and manipulate groups of objects.

2. Why Collections over Arrays?

Arrays have fixed size.

Collections grow dynamically.

3. What is ArrayList?

ArrayList is a resizable array implementation of the List interface.

4. Which package contains ArrayList?

java.util

5. How do you add elements to an ArrayList?

add()

6. How do you access elements?

get(index)

7. How do you remove elements?

remove(index)

8. How do you get size?

size()

9. Does ArrayList allow duplicates?

Yes.

10. Does ArrayList maintain insertion order?

Yes.
